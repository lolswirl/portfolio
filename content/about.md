hi again. i'm ronald, though most people online know me as _swirl_.

i'm a software engineer in new york city, and i'm always building something, at work or not.

## work

i started college early, as a dual-enrolled high school student, and finished a computer science degree at the university of north florida in 2019. in my last year there i did research in computational geometry, and presented our [paper](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-34029-2_10) at a conference in kalamata, greece.

after that i spent a couple of years at smt, building the software behind tennis tournaments, like the scheduling, the chair umpires' score entry, and the camera and radar systems. i was the onsite software lead at five grand slams. when something breaks in the middle of a live match, you learn to find the problem fast.

in 2021 i moved to macquarie, where i work on the systems traders use to book and manage credit trades. it's mostly c++ and python, and the part i like most is how close it is to the people using it. a lot of my day is on the trading floor, working through problems with traders while their trades are live.

across all of it, i'd rather build something strong and useful than something quick. i like working close to the people who use what i make, and i care about it holding up when it matters, in the middle of a live match or while a trade is going through. a lot of my favorite work started as a tool i made for myself, until the people around me started using it, and then other teams did too. seeing people get real use out of something i built is the most satisfying part of the job.

want to know more? feel free to [get in touch](#get-in-touch)! :)

## play

### world of warcraft

i've played wow since cataclysm, almost 600 days of /played across it all, and i've mained mistweaver monk since mists of pandaria. healing competitively has been my focus ever since, and i've reached world rank 20 in raid and earned 0.1% titles in mythic+ across several seasons. i write the [mistweaver guide](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/monk/mistweaver/overview-pve-healer) on wowhead, and i'm a veteran and moderator in peak of serenity, the monk community discord.

most of my side projects come from the game. it started because i had questions about my own spec that no tool answered, and that turned into [wait, i'm ramping!](/projects/waitimramping/), a site of planning and analysis tools for healers. from there i started contributing to the tools other players already use. i maintain the mistweaver module in [wowanalyzer](/projects/wowanalyzer/), and built the mistweaver model in [qe live](/projects/questionably-epic/). i also write [addons](/projects/swirladdons/), mostly small ones that fix one thing.

i like helping other healers get better and enjoy the role more.

### other games

outside of wow, i mostly play games about building and surviving, like subnautica, terraria, minecraft, don't starve, and oxygen not included. terraria is the one that stuck, at around 3,000 hours. then there are the slower games about tending to things, like stardew valley, where i've put in about 2,000 hours, and graveyard keeper.

a couple go back much further, like super meat boy and pokemon. i started pokemon on a game boy advance in kindergarten and kept going through the ds and the switch.

## home

i'm a proud cat dad of two, spook and yeti, both named after halloween, my favorite holiday. they're both long-haired. spook is a black former stray from my college days, and yeti is a white maine coon.

part of why i love that time of year is that i'm a cold weather person at heart. i love snow, and if summer stopped being a season i don't think i'd miss it. _ok, a few days of it is fine._

a lot of my time at home goes into making it feel cozy. i like modern, brutalist, and rural design, and i like them even more when they're mixed together. plants tie it all together, and i'm up to 45 of them around the apartment.

## tech

i love technology, and a lot of it ends up on my desk or around the apartment. i built a custom ethernet routing system for the apartment, i build custom keyboards (...there's always room for one more), and i'm always trying new peripherals.

i'm also really into home automation. there's a smart device for almost everything around the house now, and i like getting them all working together. a lot of that runs through my two raspberry pis. the first runs homebridge with custom plugins, so devices that don't support apple home still show up in it, along with adguard, which blocks ads for the whole network at the dns level. it also hosts [servers up](/projects/servers-up/), my discord bot for wow server status. the second runs octoprint, so i can keep an eye on my 3d printer. (i'm hoping to give the pis more to do this year!)

that printer gets a lot of use. i've printed more useful things than i can count, like plant pots, cable clips, a battery holder, lamp diffusers, parts around the house, and pieces for the pi cases. also a [shrektopus](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4949308) in rainbow filament. once you have a printer, the list of things to make never really ends.

lately i've been following the new wave of ai hardware, like ar glasses and smart rings. thinking about where all of it is taking the world, and how fast, is one of the things that excites me most.

## music

i've loved electronic dance music since i was a kid, and it never stopped. house, drum and bass, dubstep, techno, hardstyle - really no limits. as i got older, that turned into festivals, once i was finally old enough to get in and hear it live. i've been to more than 30 so far, and i have no plans of stopping.
